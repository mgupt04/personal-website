01 / The pitch

Why me.

Technology should reach the people who need it most. Not just the people who can afford it.

That conviction is why I build.

Too many good ideas die in the gap between a lab result and a real person. Assistive devices priced out of reach at $5,000. Clinical AI that no one can verify. Research that looks brilliant until someone checks the math. I work in that gap.

So I hold my research to the strictest standard I can find, and I build things people can actually use: a $30 vision wearable for blind and low-vision users, 150+ care packages for hospitalized children across three countries through a nonprofit I help lead, and a healthcare startup rethinking how patients recover after surgery.

The goal is bigger than any one project: AI that is trustworthy by design and accessible by default.
